## The mark

A violet T beam resting on a teal W drawn as two linked check marks: the standard you build to, and the path from seeing a problem to proving the fix. The open rings are checkpoints; the solid end node is the proven fix.

| File | Use |

| `icon-light.svg` | App icon on white or pale backgrounds |

| `wordmark.svg` | Name only, Inter SemiBold outlined to shapes, letter spacing −2% |

## Rules

- Clear space on every side equal to the height of the end node.
- Smallest sizes: icon 16 px, full logo 24 px tall.
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate or outline the mark, or add gradients or shadows.
- Don't separate the beam from the W, or use the W alone.
- Write the name as one word with a capital T: Trustwright.
- Use ™ until registered; ® only after registration.
